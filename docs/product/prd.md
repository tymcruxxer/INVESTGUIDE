1. Executive Summary
Vision Statement
To become Zimbabwe's leading AI-powered investment intelligence platform that empowers ordinary citizens to make smarter, data-driven financial decisions.

InvestGuide is an AI-powered Zimbabwean investment intelligence and analytics platform designed to help retail investors, young professionals, diaspora investors, SMEs, and beginner market participants make informed investment decisions. The platform bridges the gap between institutional-grade financial intelligence and everyday Zimbabwean investors by transforming fragmented market information into understandable, actionable insights.

Mission
InvestGuide exists to bridge the gap between institutional-grade financial intelligence and everyday Zimbabwean investors by transforming fragmented market information into understandable, actionable insights.

An investment intelligence platform	A brokerage or trading platform
A financial analytics platform	An automated trading bot
A market research assistant	A get-rich-quick platform

2. Problem Statement
Zimbabwean investors face five systemic challenges that InvestGuide directly addresses:

Problem	Description	InvestGuide Solution
Fragmented Information	Market data, research, and announcements scattered across many disconnected platforms	Unified intelligence platform aggregating all sources
Low Financial Literacy	Many investors do not understand stocks, REITs, bonds, or inflation-adjusted returns	AI explanations and educational hub simplify concepts
No Accessible Research Tools	Professional-grade intelligence available only to institutions	Retail-accessible analytics and dashboards
Information Overload	Retail investors struggle to interpret macro events, analyst reports, and news	AI summarization and contextual filtering
Zimbabwe's Economic Complexity	Traditional analysis fails in Zimbabwe's inflation and currency environment	Macroeconomic adjustment engine and inflation-aware analytics

6. Feature Specifications
6.1 Dashboard — Central Intelligence Hub
The main dashboard is the central intelligence hub presenting immediate market context and AI insights.

Section	Content	Purpose
Market Overview	ZSE/VFEX performance, inflation rate, exchange rates, top movers	Immediate market pulse at a glance
AI Insights Feed	AI-generated market summaries, sector analysis, macro developments	Contextual intelligence delivery
Watchlist Overview	User-tracked assets with price movement and sentiment	Personalized portfolio monitoring
Investment Opportunities	Personalized recommendations based on risk profile and goals	Actionable guidance
Market News Feed	Aggregated financial news, analyst commentary, announcements	Real-time intelligence stream

6.2 Asset Explorer
Allows users to browse, filter, compare, and discover investments across ZSE, VFEX, and REITs.

Filter dimensions: sector, risk level, dividend yield, sentiment, volatility, market cap, momentum, investment category.

6.3 Asset Detail Pages
Each asset page functions as a complete intelligence profile including:
•	Interactive historical price charts with inflation-adjusted overlays
•	AI Summary Card with plain-language analysis
•	Sentiment intelligence with institutional outlook
•	Key metrics: market cap, dividend yield, volatility, liquidity, momentum score
•	Related news and research aggregation
•	Macroeconomic exposure analysis (inflation sensitivity, currency exposure)
•	Comparison suggestions and alternative investment cards
•	Educational layer explaining metrics for beginners

6.4 AI Assistant
Conversational investment intelligence assistant that explains, summarizes, and contextualizes. Users can ask questions such as:
•	"What are the best REITs for passive income?"
•	"Compare VFEX mining stocks"
•	"How does inflation affect banking stocks?"
•	"Explain dividend yield"
Responses include streaming text, markdown rendering, inline charts, asset cards, and clickable recommendations. AI responses always cite data sources and acknowledge uncertainty.

6.5 News Intelligence Center
Financial intelligence feed covering: market news, macroeconomic news, company news, analyst reports, policy announcements, and commodity news. AI condenses long analyst reports and earnings releases into digestible summaries.

6.6 Macroeconomic Dashboard
Tracks and visualizes: inflation trends, exchange rate movements, RBZ policy indicators, gold prices, interest rates, and liquidity conditions. AI explains how macro conditions affect sectors and investment implications.

6.7 Watchlist System
Users can track assets, save comparisons, follow sectors, and receive alerts. Watchlists display sentiment shifts, AI insights, price alerts, news alerts, and macro warnings.

6.8 Alert System
Alert types: major price movements, sentiment changes, macroeconomic events, dividend announcements, AI recommendation updates. Delivery via in-app notifications and email initially, with push notifications and WhatsApp integration planned.

6.9 Educational Hub
Covers: investing basics, REITs explained, VFEX explained, dividends, inflation, risk, portfolio diversification, macroeconomics. Future: quizzes, simulations, guided investing paths.
