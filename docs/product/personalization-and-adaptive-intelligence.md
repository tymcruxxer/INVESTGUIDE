# Personalization and Adaptive Intelligence Vision

InvestGuide should feel like an investment intelligence platform that understands the investor in front of it. Personalization is not about pushing users into trades or pretending to know the future. It is about adapting education, analytics, explanations, discovery, and comparisons to each user's goals, experience, risk appetite, and investment context.

The platform remains an educational investment intelligence product, not a broker, portfolio manager, licensed advisor, or automated trading system.

## Intelligent Onboarding

Onboarding should collect enough information to make the product immediately useful without overwhelming the user. The first experience should identify what the investor is trying to learn or accomplish, then configure dashboards, explanations, education paths, and asset intelligence accordingly.

Onboarding should capture:

* experience level
* risk appetite
* investment goals
* preferred investment types
* investment horizon
* planned investment amount or range
* preferred explanation depth
* education focus areas

The onboarding flow should be lightweight, editable, and transparent. Users should understand why each question is asked and how it affects the experience.

## Investor Profile

The investor profile is the long-lived personalization object for InvestGuide. It should eventually connect to authentication, watchlists, dashboards, saved comparisons, alerts, and AI interactions.

Initial profile dimensions:

* experience level: beginner, intermediate, advanced
* risk appetite: conservative, moderate, aggressive
* investment goals: wealth building, passive income, capital preservation, inflation protection, USD exposure, education, diversification
* preferred investment types: ZSE equities, VFEX equities, REITs, bonds, money market products, alternatives
* investment horizon: short term, medium term, long term, retirement, education funding, wealth transfer
* planned investment amount or range: optional band rather than precise financial disclosure
* preferred language level: simple, balanced, technical
* education focus: dividends, REITs, VFEX, inflation, risk, valuation, macroeconomics, portfolio construction

## Investment Goals

Investment goals should shape the way InvestGuide organizes information. A passive-income investor should see dividends, REIT yield, distribution history, and income stability explained more prominently. A growth-oriented investor should see revenue growth, market expansion, sector momentum, and volatility context more clearly. A capital-preservation investor should see liquidity, drawdown risk, defensive sectors, and inflation protection.

Goals should guide education and presentation, not become automatic recommendations.

## Preferred Investment Types

Preferred investment types help the platform prioritize discovery and comparison surfaces:

* ZSE equities for local listed company exposure
* VFEX equities for USD-linked or internationally exposed assets
* REITs for property-backed income and diversification
* bonds and money market products for conservative or income-focused users
* alternatives for future education and long-term expansion

Preference does not hide the rest of the market. It changes defaults, ordering, educational prompts, and comparison suggestions.

## Investment Horizon

Investment horizon changes how risk and performance should be explained.

Shorter horizons should emphasize liquidity, volatility, near-term macro sensitivity, and capital preservation. Longer horizons should emphasize compounding, dividend reinvestment, sector durability, inflation-adjusted returns, and business quality.

## Planned Investment Amount

Planned investment amount should be captured as a range, not an exact value. It can help tailor education around diversification, fees, minimum practical position sizes, and beginner-friendly allocation concepts.

InvestGuide should avoid treating this value as permission to provide financial advice. It should inform educational framing only.

## Risk Appetite

Risk appetite should be represented clearly:

* conservative: prioritizes capital preservation, lower volatility, liquidity, income stability, and plain-language caution
* moderate: balances income, growth, diversification, and controlled volatility
* aggressive: accepts higher volatility and longer drawdowns for potential growth, while still requiring clear risk explanation

Risk appetite should affect language, visibility of risk metrics, and comparison context. It should not produce automatic buy/sell instructions.

## Experience-Level Language Adaptation

InvestGuide should adapt explanations across three levels.

Beginner language should:

* define financial terms inline
* avoid unexplained jargon
* use concrete examples
* explain why each metric matters
* prioritize education and confidence-building

Intermediate language should:

* assume basic financial literacy
* include selected metrics and tradeoffs
* connect company data to sector and macro context
* explain relationships between risk, income, and growth

Advanced language should:

* expose deeper metrics and assumptions
* use concise technical language
* support valuation, liquidity, volatility, macro sensitivity, and comparative analysis
* reduce basic definitions unless requested

## Educational Personalization

Education should be woven into the platform rather than isolated in a static learning center. Every asset page, comparison view, news item, and AI explanation can become a teaching moment.

Examples:

* beginners see metric definitions, examples, and guided prompts
* income-focused users see dividend and REIT education
* VFEX-focused users see USD exposure and currency context
* macro-sensitive users see inflation, exchange rate, and interest-rate explanations
* advanced users see expandable methodology notes and assumptions

## Asset Intelligence Pages

Asset intelligence pages should adapt to the investor profile. The same Delta, Econet, Innscor, Tigere REIT, or VFEX mining page should not feel identical for every user.

Profile-aware asset pages may adjust:

* headline summary language
* metric order
* educational callouts
* risk explanation depth
* related news emphasis
* comparison suggestions
* macro exposure explanations
* income versus growth framing

## Stock and REIT Outlook Pages

Outlook pages should combine analytics, news, macro context, and education. A stock outlook should explain company-specific drivers, sector context, risks, and relevant metrics. A REIT outlook should emphasize occupancy, distributions, property exposure, inflation linkage, liquidity, and income stability.

Outlook pages should avoid price predictions presented as certainty. They should communicate scenarios, drivers, risks, and uncertainty.

## Comparison Engine

The comparison engine should help users understand tradeoffs between assets, not simply rank them. It should support comparisons such as:

* Delta versus Innscor for consumer exposure
* Tigere REIT versus First Mutual REIT for income-focused property exposure
* ZSE equity versus VFEX equity for currency context
* dividend-focused asset versus growth-focused asset
* conservative versus aggressive asset baskets

Comparisons should adapt explanations to the user's experience level and goals.

## AI Investment Companion Vision

The AI investment companion should eventually act as a patient financial interpreter and research assistant. It should remember the investor's profile, adapt language, cite sources, explain uncertainty, and guide users through research workflows.

The companion should help answer questions like:

* What does this metric mean for someone new to investing?
* Which assets should I research if I care about passive income?
* How does inflation affect this sector?
* Compare these two REITs in simple terms.
* What should I learn before investing in VFEX shares?

The AI companion must remain educational and source-grounded. It must not promise returns, issue personalized financial advice, or replace independent research.

## Continuous Personalization

Personalization should improve over time through explicit user edits and observed product interactions. Users should always be able to review and change their profile.

Future continuous signals may include:

* assets viewed repeatedly
* sectors followed
* education topics completed
* comparison history
* saved watchlist assets
* preferred explanation depth
* AI questions asked

Continuous personalization should be transparent, reversible, privacy-conscious, and never manipulative.

## Product Principle

InvestGuide personalization should make the platform clearer, more relevant, and more educational. It should adapt the presentation of intelligence to the user, while preserving user agency, uncertainty, source grounding, and the platform's educational-not-advisory boundary.
