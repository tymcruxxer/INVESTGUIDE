# InvestGuide Product Pillars

InvestGuide has five permanent product pillars: Learn, Plan, Invest, Manage, and Grow. Every module should belong to one or more pillars and should help the user make a better financial decision.

## 1. Learn

### Purpose

Build financial literacy and confidence through contextual education. Users should understand concepts before using advanced tools.

### Users

* beginners learning stocks, REITs, VFEX, dividends, inflation, and risk
* intermediate users improving analytical fluency
* advanced users reviewing methodology and macro assumptions
* students and education-focused users

### AI Responsibilities

* explain concepts in the user's preferred language level
* translate jargon into plain language
* generate examples from Zimbabwean market context
* recommend learning paths based on goals and gaps
* cite sources and avoid advice-like conclusions

### Future Modules

* learning paths
* quizzes and knowledge checks
* financial glossary
* concept explainers inside asset pages
* simulation-based lessons
* AI tutor mode

## 2. Plan

### Purpose

Help users clarify goals, time horizon, risk appetite, savings capacity, and financial priorities before comparing investments.

### Users

* new investors deciding where to start
* young professionals planning monthly investments
* diaspora investors planning USD-linked exposure
* SME owners managing cash and treasury needs
* families planning education, emergency funds, or retirement

### AI Responsibilities

* ask clarifying questions
* summarize goals and constraints
* explain tradeoffs between horizon, risk, liquidity, and income
* suggest education topics and research workflows
* never prescribe exact financial actions as advice

### Future Modules

* goal planner
* emergency fund planner
* retirement roadmap
* investment readiness checklist
* savings and debt planning tools
* financial roadmap generator

## 3. Invest

### Purpose

Provide market, asset, news, macro, and research intelligence so users can compare investment opportunities responsibly.

### Users

* retail investors researching ZSE, VFEX, REITs, bonds, and money markets
* income-focused users comparing dividends and REIT distributions
* growth-oriented users comparing sectors and company quality
* advanced users reviewing metrics and macro exposures

### AI Responsibilities

* explain analytics outputs and uncertainty
* summarize news and research
* compare assets based on documented metrics
* surface relevant risks and assumptions
* ground every insight in data and sources

### Future Modules

* asset intelligence pages
* stock and REIT outlook pages
* comparison engine
* opportunity radar
* investment scores
* news intelligence
* macro-aware research assistant

## 4. Manage

### Purpose

Help users monitor financial positions, portfolio exposure, risk, allocation, cash needs, and learning progress over time.

### Users

* users tracking watchlists and holdings
* income investors monitoring dividends and distributions
* SME owners monitoring capital preservation choices
* advanced users reviewing diversification and risk exposure

### AI Responsibilities

* explain portfolio changes and concentration risk
* summarize exposure by asset type, sector, currency, and goal
* detect when a user's portfolio no longer matches stated goals
* explain risk in an educational, non-alarming way
* avoid automated trading or rebalancing instructions

### Future Modules

* portfolio tracking
* watchlists
* alerts
* diversification analysis
* income tracking
* goal progress dashboards
* document vault for statements and reports

## 5. Grow

### Purpose

Support long-term improvement in financial capability, investment discipline, and wealth-building behavior.

### Users

* users progressing from beginner to advanced
* long-term investors tracking goals over years
* users building healthier financial habits
* premium users seeking deeper intelligence

### AI Responsibilities

* adapt dashboards as users mature
* recommend next learning steps
* explain progress and gaps
* create long-term roadmaps
* help users revisit assumptions when circumstances change

### Future Modules

* financial health score
* long-term roadmaps
* achievement and milestone tracking
* adaptive education
* advanced simulations
* premium intelligence reports
