# Analytics Roadmap

InvestGuide analytics should help users interpret financial decisions with context. Analytics must be transparent, explainable, and grounded in reliable data. This document defines future analytics only; it does not implement calculations.

## Calculation Philosophy

* Prefer understandable models before complex black boxes.
* Show components and assumptions.
* Separate raw metrics, normalized scores, and AI explanations.
* Use Zimbabwe-specific macro context where relevant.
* Never present a score as a guaranteed outcome.

## Investment Scores

Inputs: asset data, price history, dividends, liquidity, news, macro indicators, sector context.

Interpretation: composite research signal explaining relative strength, weakness, and uncertainty.

## Financial Health Score

Inputs: savings, debt, emergency fund, portfolio, goals, income range, horizon.

Interpretation: readiness and resilience indicator, not a judgment or credit score.

## Diversification

Inputs: holdings, asset types, sectors, currencies, geographies, income sources.

Interpretation: concentration and exposure explanation.

## Risk

Inputs: volatility, drawdown, liquidity, leverage exposure, currency exposure, macro sensitivity.

Interpretation: relative risk drivers and what users should understand before acting.

## Opportunity Score

Inputs: valuation metrics, momentum, quality, sentiment, macro tailwinds, dividend context.

Interpretation: research prioritization signal, not a buy recommendation.

## Volatility

Inputs: historical returns, price frequency, liquidity, market context.

Interpretation: how much an asset has moved and what that may imply for horizon and risk tolerance.

## Quality Score

Inputs: earnings quality, balance sheet strength, governance, consistency, sector resilience.

Interpretation: business durability and reliability context.

## Dividend Score

Inputs: dividend history, payout stability, yield, cash flow indicators, REIT distributions.

Interpretation: income reliability and sustainability context.

## Income Score

Inputs: dividends, distributions, bond/money-market yields, frequency, volatility, inflation context.

Interpretation: usefulness for passive-income or cash-flow goals.

## Growth Score

Inputs: revenue trends, earnings trends, sector expansion, reinvestment, market position.

Interpretation: growth potential and uncertainty drivers.

## Value Score

Inputs: valuation ratios, asset value, earnings, dividends, peer comparison, macro adjustment.

Interpretation: whether an asset appears relatively expensive, fair, or cheaper by documented assumptions.

## Macro Score

Inputs: inflation, exchange rates, interest rates, RBZ indicators, commodity exposure, currency denomination.

Interpretation: macro tailwinds/headwinds affecting assets, sectors, and portfolios.

## News Sentiment

Inputs: normalized articles, source trust, asset links, language, sentiment model output, recency.

Interpretation: current narrative tone and relevance, not factual proof of future performance.

## Future Analytics Layers

* score confidence
* methodology pages
* benchmark comparison
* inflation-adjusted returns
* currency-adjusted returns
* scenario analysis
* portfolio risk attribution
* goal alignment scoring
* AI-generated explanation of score movement

## Safety Requirements

All analytics should show:

* data sources
* calculation date
* missing data warnings
* methodology summary
* confidence or completeness indicator
* educational disclaimer where used for decisions
