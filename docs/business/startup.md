2. Market Opportunity
2.1 Market Context
Zimbabwe's investment market is underserved by modern analytics infrastructure. Key conditions create a significant opportunity:
•	Growing retail investor interest in ZSE and VFEX markets
•	Large Zimbabwean diaspora with USD income seeking local market exposure
•	No existing AI-powered investment intelligence platform for Zimbabwean markets
•	Rising mobile and internet penetration among young professionals
•	Increasing institutional research from firms like IH Securities and MMC Capital with no retail aggregation layer
•	Zimbabwe's unique macroeconomic complexity (inflation, currency instability) creates demand for specialized intelligence

2.2 Target Market Segments
Segment	Profile	Size Indicator

3.3 Unit Economics Philosophy
InvestGuide's SaaS model is designed for low customer acquisition cost (CAC) via organic content and financial education, with high lifetime value (LTV) driven by premium subscription stickiness. Broker partnerships create an additional revenue layer without increasing user acquisition costs.

7. Legal & Compliance Positioning
7.1 Regulatory Positioning
Platform Classification
InvestGuide positions itself as an AI-powered investment intelligence and education platform. It is NOT a licensed broker, automated investment advisor, portfolio manager, or guaranteed-return service.

7.2 Compliance Language Rules
Avoid	Use Instead
"Guaranteed returns"	"May suit income-focused investors"
"Best stock to buy now"	"Data suggests strong momentum"
"You will profit"	"Historically resilient"
"AI predicts markets accurately"	"Market sentiment indicates"
"Safe investment"	"Based on available information"

7.3 Standard Disclaimer
Platform Disclaimer
InvestGuide provides educational investment intelligence and analytics. This platform does not provide licensed financial advice. Always conduct independent research before investing.

 
8. Trust Architecture
8.1 Trust Design Principles
•	Transparency — Always explain data sources, methodology, and uncertainty
•	Explainability — Every recommendation includes clear reasoning
•	Source ranking — Official > Institutional > Journalism > General web
•	AI safety — Grounded responses only, no hallucinated financial data
•	Freshness indicators — All data displays last-updated timestamps
•	Confidence indicators — AI insights include certainty levels

8.2 Source Trust Hierarchy
Tier	Sources	Trust Level
Tier 1 — Official	ZSE, VFEX, RBZ, ZIMSTAT	Highest — always preferred
Tier 2 — Institutional	IH Securities, MMC Capital, Old Mutual	High — cited prominently
Tier 3 — Journalism	Financial Gazette, NewsDay Business	Moderate — verified and summarized
Tier 4 — General Web	Community sources, general publications	Low — marked clearly, used cautiously

10. Key Risks & Mitigations
Risk	Description	Mitigation
Data quality risk	Incorrect financial data destroys trust quickly	Validation systems, anomaly detection, tiered sources
AI hallucination risk	Fabricated financial information misleads users	RAG architecture, source grounding, validation layer
Regulatory risk	Financial platform regulatory scrutiny	Clear non-broker positioning, disclaimer system
Scraper reliability risk	Source websites change structure or block scrapers	Modular scrapers, fallback systems, monitoring
Adoption risk	Low initial user adoption in target market	Education-first content, free tier, diaspora focus
Competitive risk	International platforms expanding to Zimbabwe	Deep local intelligence moat, community trust
